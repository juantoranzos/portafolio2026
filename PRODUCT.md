# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Businesses and individuals evaluating a Full Stack developer before initiating contact. Their job: form a confident read of Juan's skill level, professional judgment, and availability — then reach out. Secondary audience: recruiters and technical leads scanning for concrete evidence of competence.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Juan Toranzos, Full Stack Developer (React / Next.js) based in Tucumán, Argentina. Exists to convert qualified visitors into project inquiries. Success = a real client message via the contact form or direct email. Every design decision serves that conversion.

## Brand Personality

Engineered. Precise. Confident.

A developer from an architecture family who builds for construction and mining clients. The aesthetic parallels the work: structural thinking, disciplined execution, nothing gratuitous. The portfolio is a professional statement, not a creative experiment.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolio: purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism cards, animated mesh blobs, acid-green on black.
- SaaS landing page: big-number-plus-label hero, stats section, infinite testimonial carousels.
- AI-portfolio monoculture: gradient text, neon accents, animated particle backgrounds.
- "Startup" aesthetic: rounded blobs, cheerful illustrations, gradient CTA buttons.
- Generic Klim-clone editorial: italic serif drop caps, three-column ruled grid as aesthetic default.

## Design Principles

1. **Blueprint precision**: the drafting-table aesthetic is a conceptual through-line tied to the client's background and industries served — not surface decoration.
2. **Content earns accent**: the amber accent appears only on real metrics, active states, and the one key accent moment per section. Never flooded.
3. **Typography carries hierarchy**: scale, weight, and tracking establish structure before color enters.
4. **Structural annotation as signal**: mono coordinate labels and section indices function as actual technical notation — they are used only where order or structure genuinely carries meaning.
5. **Show, don't list**: one real outcome (+45% sales) outweighs a ten-point feature list.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. All text at ≥4.5:1 contrast on their backgrounds. Visible 2px accent focus indicators on every interactive element. Full keyboard navigation. prefers-reduced-motion: all transforms, the marquee, and magnetic effects freeze to final state.
